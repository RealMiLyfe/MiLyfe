# MiScale OS — Growth, Evolution, Capacity, and Value Continuity

**Status:** Design specification
**Type:** Full independently deployable OS
**Relationship:** Integrates with MiForge, MiOps, MiMoney, MiTreasury, MiMarket, MiWork, MiDevice, MiData, MiKnowledge, MiMedia, Governance, Campaign, and MiPublic Office
**Pilot context:** Jacksonville/Duval first place cell
**No code:** This document defines ownership, operations, integrations, UI, gates, and acceptance criteria only.

## 1. Purpose

`MiScale OS` is the platform growth, evolution, capacity, replication, and value-continuity system.

It turns successful MiForge ventures, services, agents, device contributions, transactions, community work, media, knowledge, and local activation into measured, governed growth without making growth itself a source of hidden control.

MiScale answers:

- What is growing?
- Why is it growing?
- Is the growth financially settled or only projected?
- Is there enough service, support, storage, energy, and governance capacity?
- What should be activated next?
- What should be paused, rolled back, or retired?
- Who owns the value created?
- How does the value survive the departure of a founder, operator, campaign, or vendor?
- Can the system replicate safely into another cell?

## 2. Boundary law

### MiScale owns

- Growth strategy and stage gates
- Capacity planning
- Activation planning
- Replication readiness
- OS adoption and deprecation planning
- Evolution proposals
- Version and migration coordination
- Revenue and contribution attribution at the platform-growth level
- Growth reserves requested from MiTreasury
- Value-continuity records
- Growth experiments and feature-rollout governance
- Cross-OS growth metrics
- Scale-related incidents and corrective plans

### MiScale does not own

- Money issuance, balances, or settlement — `MiMoney`
- Treasury custody or final allocation — `MiTreasury` and authorized owners
- Business creation — `MiForge`
- Day-to-day reliability — `MiOps`
- Personal growth and life development — existing `MiGrowth`
- Human labor claims — `MiWork`
- Device ownership or physical control — `MiDevice`
- Campaign authority — `Campaign OS`
- Election results or office authority — `MiElection` and `MiPublic Office`
- Platform governance — `Governance OS`
- Court or constitutional outcomes — `MiJustice`

`MiGrowth` remains the human and community development OS. `MiScale` is the platform and network evolution OS. They must not be merged.

## 3. MiForge-to-MiScale economic flywheel

The intended first-income strategy is represented as a governed flywheel rather than an unverified promise of revenue.

1. **Identify an opportunity** through community need, market demand, place problems, campaign priorities, or platform capacity.
2. **Forge a venture or service** through MiForge: business, agent, workflow, media product, education service, marketplace offer, or white-label system.
3. **Define the value contract**: owner, contributors, customer, deliverable, price, quality standard, costs, risks, rights, and support obligation.
4. **Activate a bounded pilot** with explicit capacity and spend limits.
5. **Deliver verified value** through MiMarket, MiWork, MiDevice, MiMedia, MiEducation, MiPlace, or another domain OS.
6. **Record actual settlement** through MiMoney. Projected income is never treated as received income.
7. **Reconcile costs and obligations** through MiClear, MiBooks, MiTax, MiCover, and relevant legal profiles.
8. **Allocate settled value** through approved MiTreasury policies: contributors, device or power contributors, operating reserve, maintenance, community benefit, UBI capacity, and reinvestment.
9. **Measure outcomes** through MiScale, MiOps, MiData, and privacy-preserving analytics.
10. **Expand, adapt, pause, or retire** using stage gates.

Rewards must be tied to verified contribution, delivery, ownership, or approved community work. The design must not depend on recruitment-only rewards, hidden fees, unbounded promises, or negative balances. MiLegal reviews external classifications and campaign, employment, tax, financial, and consumer obligations.

## 4. Value-continuity law

To make value survive any individual leaving, the system maintains a **Value Continuity Register**.

Each value-producing object records:

- Value ID
- Creating OS or MiForge venture
- Owner
- Steward
- Contributors
- Contribution receipts
- License or usage rights
- Revenue-share or reward policy
- Maintenance obligation
- Dependency list
- Data and records location
- Successor plan
- Community or cooperative claim, if any
- Transfer restrictions
- Version history
- Fork behavior
- Shutdown behavior

This register does not claim ownership over external property. It records the actual internal rights, contracts, receipts, and responsibilities that participants choose to create.

## 6. Scale stages

### Stage 0 — Seed

MiForge produces the first useful venture, service, workflow, or product. The goal is not broad activation; it is proving that a real person or organization receives real value and that the value can be recorded.

### Stage 1 — Verified value

The offer has repeatable delivery, clear ownership, contribution receipts, settled economics, support procedures, and a safe rollback.

### Stage 2 — Jacksonville pilot

The offer runs inside the Duval/Jacksonville cell with local data, support, community feedback, and capacity limits. Expansion requires evidence rather than enthusiasm alone.

### Stage 3 — Cell density

Multiple households, organizations, cooperatives, and services use the same Kernel contracts. The platform measures whether support, devices, infrastructure, and governance can keep up.

### Stage 4 — Replication

A second cell can activate the offer from an exportable package without hidden founder access or dependence on the original operator.

### Stage 5 — Federation

Cells exchange only explicitly permitted capabilities, events, data, and settlements. This stage remains optional until the pilot proves local safety and exit rights.

## 7. Activation gates

An OS, venture, device program, or cell cannot move to the next stage until the gate is satisfied.

### Economic gate

- Revenue or funding source is identified.
- Settled and projected funds are separate.
- Costs and obligations are recorded.
- Reward policy is approved.
- Reserve requirement is met.
- No negative balances are required.

### Capacity gate

- Compute, storage, network, device, power, support, and governance capacity are measured.
- The next stage has a capacity owner.
- Failure and degraded-mode behavior are documented.

### Safety gate

- Threat model exists.
- Device and agent scopes are bounded.
- Child and vulnerable-person risks are reviewed.
- Human approval requirements are active.
- Pause and rollback work.

### Value gate

- The offer delivers a verifiable benefit.
- Ownership and contributor records are complete.
- Users can leave and export.
- The value does not depend on one person retaining hidden keys.

### Governance gate

- Decision rights are visible.
- Conflicts of interest are recorded.
- Community or owner approval is complete where required.
- MiResolve, MiLegal, and MiJustice routes are defined.

## 9. Device and power contribution design

Device participation is opt-in and owner-authorized.

The device contribution record includes:

- Device identity
- Owner
- Approved workload
- Start and end time
- Work completed
- Resource class
- Power or resource estimate
- Local safety limits
- Privacy classification
- Verification method
- Contribution receipt
- Reward status
- Revocation status

No device may be enrolled into productive work without an owner-approved scope. MiDevice and MiGuard enforce pause, thermal, battery, network, privacy, and physical safety rules.

Power or compute rewards are issued only after contribution verification and MiMoney settlement. MiScale does not manufacture an unearned balance.

## 10. Evolution system

MiScale includes an Evolution Register for:

- New OS versions
- Schema changes
- Capability changes
- Feature flags
- Staged rollouts
- Migrations
- Deprecations
- Forks
- Policy changes
- Service substitutions
- Vendor replacement

Every change has:

- Reason
- Owner
- Impacted OSes
- Data migration plan
- Permission impact
- Financial impact
- Safety impact
- Rollback plan
- Human or community approval
- Effective date
- Receipt

### Feature rollout

Use feature flags for safe activation, not to hide authority changes. Flags must have owners, expiration dates, audit history, targeting rules, and a kill switch.

Recommended open-source options:

- `open-feature/flagd` for a lightweight OpenFeature-compliant rollout service; the project describes deterministic targeting, progressive rollout, telemetry, and Apache 2.0 licensing. [flagd](https://github.com/open-feature/flagd)
- `growthbook/growthbook` for later experiments, feature flags, and product analytics; the public repository identifies the project as MIT-licensed and self-hostable. [GrowthBook](https://github.com/growthbook/growthbook)

Do not run both until there is a real need. Start with one flag system.

## 11. Growth analytics and privacy

Growth measurement must not become surveillance.

The analytics contract records:

- Purpose
- Event name
- Data minimization rule
- Consent requirement
- Retention
- Aggregation level
- Owner
- Export behavior
- Deletion behavior

Possible open-source extensions:

- Matomo for privacy-oriented website analytics, with a license review of core versus premium plugins before use. [Matomo](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)
- Prometheus/Grafana for service and capacity metrics
- MiData event tables for authoritative platform outcomes

Core growth decisions should use settled, permissioned data rather than advertising profiles or hidden behavioral scoring.

## 12. Campaign integration: Jax / mayor campaign

The campaign is represented as a Campaign OS instance whose public website is the replaceable channel `mijaxx.fun`.

The current public identity and message can be configured as campaign content rather than hardcoded into the backend. The current reference site presents:

- Mayor MiLyfe — Jacksonville 2027
- Candidate/campaign principal: Carnell Lee
- “We The People” positioning
- Jacksonville and Duval place scope
- Platform and research pages
- Petition and get-involved flows
- Campaign calendar
- Public disclosures
- Candidate journey and biography
- Public media
- MiLyfe join path

The system must not treat the campaign objective as a guaranteed election result. It records campaign goals, deadlines, filings, signatures, outreach, and public commitments. Election authority remains with the applicable election system and lawful authorities.

### Campaign-to-growth integrations

- Campaign needs and platform proposals can create MiForge opportunity requests.
- MiKnowledge supplies source-linked research.
- MiMedia supplies approved campaign media.
- MiAdSpace provides consent-based promotion.
- MiMarket can publish eligible offers.
- MiMoney records contributions and settlements.
- MiBooks and MiTax record financial obligations and disclosures.
- MiScale measures capacity and outcomes without giving Campaign OS control over MiLyfe's core treasury or ledger.

Campaign data remains separated from public-office data. If an elected office is activated, the transition creates a new public-office identity and permission set.

## 15. MiScale support, disputes, and legal paths

- Service or reward disagreement inside MiLyfe → `MiResolve`
- Contract, tax, licensing, or jurisdiction question → `MiLegal`
- Rights, court, government accountability, class action, restitution, or incarceration issue → `MiJustice`
- Device safety incident → `MiGuard`, `MiDevice`, and `MiEmergency`
- Financial settlement dispute → MiMoney/Clear plus the applicable dispute route

## 16. Definition of done

MiScale is ready for the Jacksonville pilot when:

- A MiForge venture can be proposed, reviewed, activated, measured, paused, and retired.
- Projected and settled income are separate.
- Contributions and rewards have verifiable receipts.
- Device and power contributions are opt-in and revocable.
- MiMoney remains the only authoritative ledger.
- MiTreasury controls actual allocations.
- No reward requires negative balances or hidden recruitment dependency.
- Capacity is measured before expansion.
- A new cell can receive a replication package without founder-only access.
- Value, ownership, contributors, licenses, and maintenance obligations remain visible.
- Campaign growth data does not control core governance or money.
- The system continues to work after any person leaves.
