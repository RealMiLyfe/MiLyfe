# MiLyfe Legal and Public-Document Register

**Status:** Design and review register
**Product:** MiLyfe
**Mode:** No code; not legal advice
**Purpose:** Identify the documents and legal review packages needed before MiLyfe publishes its white paper, architecture blueprint, founder story, software, devices, services, campaign content, economic systems, and public pilot.

## 1. Important status statement

MiLyfe can be designed as a voluntary, people-owned network and commons without making incorporation or state registration a precondition of the technical design.

That does not mean the network is automatically exempt from external law. Legal duties may arise from what MiLyfe or its participants do, including:

- Accepting money
- Operating a campaign
- Publishing personal information
- Providing health or care services
- Employing or contracting with people
- Selling goods or services
- Operating devices
- Hosting user content
- Handling children’s data
- Providing financial or payment functions
- Using third-party intellectual property
- Interacting with public institutions

The documents below are a working register for qualified legal review, not a conclusion that any particular entity, license, registration, exemption, or legal status applies.

## 2. Document status labels

Every document is labeled:

- **Concept** — idea or design intention; not a promise
- **Draft** — being reviewed; not ready for public reliance
- **Approved for publication** — reviewed for scope, attribution, privacy, and claims
- **Binding terms** — intended to create contractual terms where legally enforceable
- **Policy** — operational rules that may be updated
- **Disclosure** — explains risks, limitations, conflicts, or external relationships
- **Record** — preserves history, consent, approval, or evidence
- **Restricted** — not public

White papers and architecture documents must not be presented as binding contracts unless separately adopted as terms.

## 3. Core public-document package

### 3.1 MiLyfe white paper

The white paper should contain:

- MiLyfe purpose
- People-owned network model
- Kernel, OS, cell, and federation model
- MiForge and MiScale model
- `$MLY` value and settlement distinctions
- Projected, pending, settled, allocated, rewarded, and reinvested states
- Device contribution rules
- Agent and skill rules
- Governance and human authority
- Security and privacy principles
- Data ownership and export
- Campaign and public-office separation
- Jacksonville/Duval pilot boundaries
- Risks and limitations
- No guaranteed income statement
- No guaranteed election-result statement
- No unsupported sovereignty or immunity statement
- No promise of financial appreciation
- No promise that projected value will settle
- Version and date
- Author and contributor attribution
- Contact and correction route

Legal review should check that the white paper does not accidentally become an investment solicitation, financial-promotion document, campaign promise, regulated service representation, or guarantee.

### 3.2 MiLyfe architecture blueprint

The blueprint should state:

- Design status
- Non-binding architecture intent
- Source of authority for each OS
- Data and permission boundaries
- Security assumptions
- External dependencies
- Third-party projects and licenses
- Experimental versus production features
- What is not yet implemented
- How changes are approved
- How users and communities exit
- How a cell forks or migrates
- How legal, financial, health, campaign, and public-office relationships are handled

It must never imply that a design diagram itself creates legal authority.

### 3.3 Founder story

The founder story should be handled as a separately reviewed narrative asset.

Needed review items:

- Authorship
- Copyright ownership or license
- Name and likeness consent
- Biography accuracy
- Statements about other people
- Statements about organizations
- Health, trauma, family, criminal, or financial disclosures
- Defamation and privacy review
- Quoted material and source permissions
- Photographs, recordings, music, and artwork permissions
- AI editing or narration disclosure
- Correction procedure
- Withdrawal or update policy

The founder story may explain origin and motivation. It must not be the sole source of governance authority, financial promises, historical facts, or public-office claims.

- Housing and public services
- Device and physical-world activity
- Public records and public-office activity

- Separate opinion from fact

## 17. Minimum legal publication gate

The following cannot be marked public until reviewed for scope, accuracy, attribution, and privacy:

- White paper
- Architecture blueprint
- Founder story
- Configuration manual
- Security manual
- MiMoney documentation
- Device contribution terms
- Agent policy
- Campaign website
- Public-office material
- Repository source
- Design assets
- Media assets

Every published document displays:

- Version
- Date
- Status
- Author or source
- License or usage terms
- Correction route
- Contact route
- Whether it is binding, policy, disclosure, or concept

## 18. Final principle

MiLyfe does not need to pretend to be something it is not. It can honestly publish its vision, design, founder story, software, and community rules as a people-owned living network while clearly identifying which activities create external legal, financial, professional, contractual, political, or public-record obligations.

The legal register protects that honesty. It does not turn MiLyfe into a state or require one central entity to own the whole network.
