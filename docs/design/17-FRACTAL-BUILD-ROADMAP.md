# MiLyfe Fractal of All Fractals Build Roadmap

**Status:** Design roadmap
**Mode:** Architecture, governance, sequencing, operations, and pilot activation only. No implementation code.
**Primary pilot:** Duval County / Jacksonville, Florida
**World shell:** `milyfe.fun`
**Political campaign channel:** `mijaxx.fun` under Campaign OS

## 1. Roadmap objective

Build MiLyfe as a recursive network of independent OSes that can operate alone, connect through shared Kernel primitives, and scale from a person or household to a business, cooperative, community, place, public office, and optional federation.

The roadmap is not a plan to build one giant application. It is a sequence for establishing:

1. Authority
2. Identity and data control
3. Integration contracts
4. Economic independence
5. Intelligence and communication
6. Governance and justice
7. Physical-world operations
8. Jacksonville pilot readiness
9. Safe replication and federation

Each wave must pass its exit gate before the next wave is activated.

## 2. Non-negotiable build laws

- No code is implied by this document; each wave first produces design, governance, operating, and acceptance artifacts.
- Kernel is implemented before dependent OSes are declared ready.
- Governance follows Kernel authority rules and cannot bypass human reserved actions.
- MiResolve never absorbs court, constitutional, government, criminal, civil-rights, class-action, or incarceration work.
- MiLegal remains neutral legal infrastructure.
- MiJustice owns rights defense, courts, government accountability, class actions, incarceration access, appeals, restitution, Redress, and Inside.
- MiMoney remains authoritative for `$MLY` issuance, balances, settlement, treasury, and economic rules.
- No parallel `$MLY` ledger is created by any OS.
- MiLyfe does not secretly hold or centrally control every person’s `$MLY`.
- Campaign, election administration, public office, and MiLyfe governance remain separated.
- Offline/local-first behavior, encryption, accessibility, child safety, exportability, and fork behavior are design requirements, not later enhancements.
- Every OS must be independently removable without destroying unrelated OSes.

## 3. The build unit: one fractal cell

Every build wave produces or upgrades a cell with:

- Kernel
- Entity registry
- OS registry
- Local data spaces
- Local event and receipt log
- Capability map
- Support and dispute routes
- Offline and recovery plan
- Bridge manifest
- Export, removal, and fork plan

The Jacksonville pilot is the first complete place cell. Personal, household, organization, business, cooperative, and community cells are created inside or alongside it using the same primitives.

## 13. Wave 7 — Jacksonville/Duval pilot cell

### Purpose

Demonstrate the complete architecture in one place before broader replication.

### Pilot boundaries

- Jacksonville/Duval is the first complete place cell.
- The pilot is not allowed to claim that it replaces government, courts, banks, election authorities, or licensed professionals.
- External institutions are connected through transparent adapters and jurisdiction profiles.
- Personal, community, campaign, business, and public-office data remain separated.

### Pilot activation sequence

1. Pilot constitution and community authority map
2. Kernel and local identity
3. Data and consent spaces
4. Support and safety operations
5. MiMoney and cooperative economic sandbox
6. Education, work, health, care, and belonging services
7. Knowledge and media publishing
8. Campaign channel integration, including `mijaxx.fun`
9. Election-information and public-office profiles where lawfully applicable
10. Place, mobility, supply, and emergency services
11. Impact measurement and independent review
12. Export, pause, removal, and fork exercise

### Jacksonville acceptance evidence

- People can join, leave, and export their data.
- No participant receives a negative `$MLY` balance.
- No single operator can silently take over every OS.
- High-impact actions require the right approval.
- Disputes route correctly to MiResolve, MiLegal, or MiJustice.
- Media, knowledge, campaign, and public-office records remain distinguishable.
- Offline and outage procedures work.
- Accessibility and child-safety reviews pass.
- The cell can be paused or forked without destroying unrelated cells.

## 14. Wave 8 — Replication and optional federation

This wave is not required for the initial Jacksonville activation.

- Capability negotiation

- Cross-cell event exchange
- Policy-profile exchange
- Data-sharing contracts
- Conflict resolution
- Cross-cell receipts
- Node suspension and appeal
- Cell departure
- Cell rejoining
- Fork creation
- Asset, data, and balance separation after a fork

### Federation law

- Participation is voluntary.
- A federation cannot silently become the owner of a cell’s people, data, devices, assets, or `$MLY`.
- Local cells may restrict or revoke bridges.
- External legal and institutional requirements are represented through jurisdiction profiles, not hidden assumptions.
- No global activation occurs until the pilot demonstrates safe local operation and clean export.

## 17. Final architecture definition

MiLyfe is complete when:

- Every important responsibility has one clear authority.
- No two OSes silently own the same source of truth.
- Every OS can work alone and connect through contracts.
- Every action has scope, approval, receipt, and recovery behavior.
- People and communities retain ownership and exit rights.
- `$MLY` has one authoritative monetary authority.
- Campaigns, elections, public offices, governance, courts, and legal infrastructure remain separate.
- Mi can act, but cannot exceed its granted scope.
- Jacksonville can operate as the first complete place cell.
- Later cells can replicate the architecture without copying hidden central control.

That is the fractal of all fractals: the same accountable primitives repeating at every scale, with local autonomy, clear authority, shared interoperability, and no requirement that one central entity own the entire world.
