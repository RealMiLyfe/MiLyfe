# MiLyfe Economy, MiMarket, Business Nodes, and Controlled Scale Strategy

**Status:** Design authority
**Product:** MiLyfe
**Mode:** Design only; no implementation code
**Pilot:** Jacksonville/Duval, with later cell replication

## 1. Purpose

MiForge is one monetization engine inside MiLyfe. It is not the entire economy.

MiLyfe needs a complete economic system where people, merchants, businesses, cooperatives, service providers, drivers, creators, organizations, agents, devices, and communities can create verified value and exchange it through bounded, transparent marketplace rails.

The economic system must:

- Create real-world usefulness before creating financial complexity.
- Make businesses and merchants easy to join.
- Make people and businesses discoverable without exposing private data.
- Support services, goods, rides, local classifieds, bookings, jobs, subscriptions, media, education, and community offers.
- Separate projected value from pending, settled, allocated, rewarded, and reinvested value.
- Support USD-facing and `$MLY`-facing flows only through approved rails.
- Scale automatically where safe and pause automatically where capacity, security, legal, or reserve conditions fail.
- Keep high-impact capabilities built but dormant until their activation gates pass.

## 5. Universal business onboarding

A business can enter through:

- MiLyfe home
- MiForge
- MiMarket
- MiJaxx campaign content, where lawfully relevant
- A local cell
- A merchant invitation
- A QR code
- A public service listing

All paths use `MiOnboard OS` and converge on the same business-node record.

### Business onboarding flow

1. Choose `Business`, `Merchant`, `Service Provider`, `Driver`, `Creator`, `Organization`, or `Venture`.
2. Sign in or create a MiLyfe identity.
3. Create or connect a business node.
4. Identify owner, steward, and representatives.
5. Select operating category and service area.
6. Provide required verification based on risk and jurisdiction.
7. Configure catalog, services, capacity, pricing, hours, and policies.
8. Configure payout and settlement options that are currently authorized.
9. Choose whether `$MLY` is accepted, offered, or unavailable.
10. Complete safety, tax, insurance, licensing, or professional review where required.
11. Publish a draft listing.
12. Pass the relevant activation gate.
13. Go live in a limited cell or marketplace category.
14. Receive the first verified order, booking, or contribution receipt.

### Business modes

- **Unverified draft** — private setup only
- **Identity-verified** — public profile with limited activity
- **Merchant-ready** — can accept orders or bookings
- **Settlement-ready** — can receive approved payouts
- **`$MLY`-enabled** — may use approved `$MLY` rails
- **Regulated-service ready** — professional or regulated review complete
- **Multi-cell ready** — can operate in additional cells
- **Partner-ready** — approved for external integrations

## 7. USD and `$MLY` rails

### 7.1 Authority

- MiMoney owns the authoritative `$MLY` ledger.
- External USD movement uses an approved payment or financial adapter.
- MiMarket records the commercial order or booking.
- MiForge records the venture or product.
- MiScale records capacity, growth, and replication.
- MiBooks records accounting and reconciliation.
- MiTax records applicable tax workflows.

### 7.2 Important activation condition

MiLyfe must not assume that a claim of legal ability automatically makes every USD-to-`$MLY` or `$MLY`-to-USD flow permissible.

Before enabling conversion, the actual structure must pass qualified legal, financial, accounting, tax, consumer-protection, anti-fraud, sanctions, and payment-partner review for the relevant jurisdiction and activity.

The design can be ready in advance, but the rail remains locked until its activation gate passes.

## 8. Value-state vocabulary

Every economic surface must distinguish:

- **Projected** — an estimate or forecast; not earned
- **Pending** — recorded but not yet settled
- **Verified** — contribution or performance evidence accepted
- **Settled** — authoritative obligation completed
- **Allocated** — assigned by an approved rule but not necessarily paid
- **Rewarded** — a reward event has been issued under approved terms
- **Reinvested** — value has been directed into a new approved activity
- **Reserved** — held for a stated obligation or risk
- **Reversed** — previously recorded value was corrected
- **Disputed** — outcome is under review

The current MiForge matching concept must use these states. A match is not earned merely because someone signs up, and projected future revenue is not settled value.

## 11. Controlled scaling and automatic unlocking

The platform can be built ahead of demand, but capability activation is gated.

### Capability states

1. Designed
2. Built
3. Disabled
4. Private development
5. Internal alpha
6. Cell pilot
7. Limited public
8. Multi-cell
9. Federated
10. Retired

### Activation conditions

A capability may advance only when the following are satisfied:

- Demand threshold
- Capacity threshold
- Uptime target
- Support coverage
- Security status
- Fraud rate
- Dispute rate
- Reserve requirement
- Settlement performance
- Legal review
- Privacy review
- Accessibility review
- Operator readiness
- Rollback readiness
- Data migration readiness
- Human governance approval where required

### Automatic behavior

MiScale may automatically:

- Prepare a configuration
- Recommend activation
- Increase safe infrastructure capacity
- Open a limited queue
- Reduce load
- Pause overloaded categories
- Apply rate limits
- Route to another healthy node
- Revert low-risk presentation or capacity changes

MiScale may not automatically unlock or expand, solely from growth metrics:

- USD-to-`$MLY` conversion
- New custody behavior
- New money authority
- Identity scope
- Campaign or election authority
- Public-office authority
- Health or legal authority
- Child-data access
- Physical-device control
- High-risk agent tools

Those require explicit authorized approval.

## 13. AI and human business operating model

MiLyfe is operated by people and bounded AI roles together.

### Operating triad

Three bounded Mi roles may act together as one coordinated operating system:

1. **MiTruth** — evidence, records, receipts, source data, and current system state.
2. **MiAdvisor** — strategy, market analysis, forecasts, scenarios, and recommendations.
3. **MiOperator** — approved execution, workflow coordination, capacity actions, and follow-through.

A fourth role, **MiGuardian**, may review security, privacy, safety, fraud, and policy risks.

These are not four unrestricted personalities. They are separate role-scoped agents with:

- Distinct permissions
- Distinct evidence requirements
- Distinct logs
- Distinct failure modes
- Distinct human escalation rules

### AI advisory board

The AI advisory board may include bounded advisors for:

- Strategy and growth
- Finance and reserves
- Marketplace and customer experience
- Security and privacy
- Legal and compliance preparation
- Operations and capacity
- Product and technology
- Community and human impact
- Local Jacksonville/Duval conditions
- Workforce and contributor health

Each advisor provides:

- Recommendation
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Confidence
- Dissent or counterargument
- Risk level
- Required human decision
- Expiration date

The AI board advises. Humans and authorized communities approve reserved actions.

### Human roles

- Founder and product steward
- Community representatives
- Finance and treasury stewards
- Security lead
- Legal and compliance counsel
- Marketplace operations
- Customer and merchant support
- Local cell operators
- Independent reviewers
- Elected or public-office personnel where applicable

## 15. Definition of economic readiness

MiLyfe is ready for a controlled economy pilot when:

- MiForge has a real paid offer with clear terms.
- MiMarket can onboard businesses and merchants.
- People can discover and use local goods and services.
- Business and campaign identities remain separated.
- USD-facing rails are handled by approved payment structures.
- `$MLY` state is authoritative and clearly labeled.
- No page copy creates balances or financial promises.
- Device contributions are verified.
- Rewards are not recruitment-only.
- Refunds, disputes, and chargebacks work.
- Security and legal gates can pause an offer.
- Capacity can scale without unlocking unsafe authority.
- Humans can override, pause, correct, and audit AI actions.
- Every business, order, booking, contribution, and settlement has a receipt.

## 16. Final strategy

MiForge is the first monetization engine, not the only one.

MiMarket becomes the economic circulation layer. MiOnboard makes people and businesses join through one consistent path. MiMoney provides the authoritative settlement layer. MiScale controls capacity and staged growth. MiSecurity, MiLegal, MiDev, MiOps, and human governance keep the economy from scaling faster than its safety, reserves, support, or legal readiness.

The platform can be built fully behind capability walls today and opened gradually as real demand, capacity, security, legal readiness, and community trust are demonstrated.
